# TC-113: Observe original selected-model quantity values and admission

## Description

Exercise separately reachable source and native quantity cells of public TC-187
U36, U38 and U40 through their original selected models. Bind inputs and expected
results by reference to the public vector API. A successful admission establishes
only admission; a partial observation never qualifies an entire mixed row.

## Test Procedure

1. Supply original nonempty model documents for acme/rpc, acme/flow and, where
   required, acme/clock. Parse original source, admit the selected documents and
   assemble declarations through public QSL APIs. Retain actual unit and dimension
   identities rather than inventing structural keys.
2. Execute U36 call admission and independent argument, sum, comparison and direct
   conversion refusals. Evaluate its original closed quotient and product bodies,
   and the entire Dur bridge let body. Compare native outcomes, canonical unit
   terms, dimension maps, exact losses, ordered charges and all counters at the
   same operation boundary with fresh meters.
3. Probe U38 conversion from three R seconds to F seconds and U40 conversion from
   three R seconds to G milliseconds before claiming equivalence-aware values.
   Preserve unexpected typed refusals as owner evidence; keep the intended
   positive cells Planned. Do not substitute temporal conversion or normalize
   owner units in integration.
4. Independently assess equivalence ratio spellings, three-root classes,
   conflicting cycles, missing mass units and invalid derived ratios. Retain full
   typed refusal payloads and original source spans. Evaluate remaining positive
   cells only through genuine owner support.
5. Generated comparisons require original emitted bytes and the authorized shared
   native runner. Source and native evidence alone gives no generated credit.

## Expected Results

Eleven focused tests pass. The source calls and refusal loci, four native
incompatibility refusals, four closed product/quotient stages, and the original
whole let bridge are observed independently. The five successful quantity stages
compare complete typed results, empty exact losses, ordered charge records and
every meter counter. Compound terms and dimension maps use original owner keys.
The let binding is observed as magnitude three with the one original root term;
the entire QSL let stage agrees with the native multiply/divide/convert sequence.

The two explicitly named characterization tests record a measured owner
disagreement: the original equivalent conversion source refuses checking with
`IllTyped(TypeMismatch)` at the conversion body, and the native context converter
refuses `IllTyped { cause: IncompatibleDimensions }` with no admitted charges and
all counters zero. The admitted classes still identify the original dimension
keys with exact root-size ratios one and one thousand respectively. These are
characterizations, not successful conversion qualifications. Intended positive
red probes were executed before characterizations were authorized.
